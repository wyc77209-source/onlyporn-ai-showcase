## English

**OnlyPorn AI** is an 18+ streaming platform for AI-produced short dramas — story-first, tasteful, character-driven. The launch series *Teacher, Long Time No See* (《老师，你好久不见》) follows Xian Min (31), an investment analyst, who reunites with Zhi Ying (39), his former teacher turned bookstore owner, ten years after graduation. Intimacy is implied only.

### Features
- 18+ age gate, cookie/privacy notices, 18 U.S.C. 2257 statement
- Series catalog, character pages, stills & storyboards; continue watching, My List, new-episode alerts
- SoftPaywall: EP01 free, 18-second soft previews on locked episodes
- Membership ladder plus per-episode **Charge to unlock** (Bilibili-style pay-per-episode / tips with a supporter badge)
- Crypto checkout (USDT / USDC with automatic on-chain verification), optional card rails
- Mobile-first UI, offline downloads (Studio), ops dashboard (daily metrics, orders)

### Pricing
| Plan | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $9.9 / mo | EP01–EP03 of each series, up to 480p |
| **Plus — RECOMMENDED** | **$19.9 / mo** | Every regular episode, up to 1080p, new ones on release day |
| Studio | $199 / yr | Everything in Plus + up to 4K + early access + offline |

### Charge to unlock
- Regular episodes: $1.99 unlocks the episode permanently · $4.99 / $9.99 charge + tip
- Studio early-access episodes: from $4.99 to unlock that episode permanently · $9.99 / $19.99 with tip
- Charges never change your membership; tip tiers earn a supporter badge on your account

### Tech stack
Next.js 15 (App Router) · React 19 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Prisma 6 · Bun · Docker; AI video / voice production pipeline (image-to-video, TTS, automated rough cuts).
